  • Demo

    BookIt Lusaka

    A mobile-first booking page in HTML, CSS and JavaScript for Lusaka salons, clinics and barbers. It is a static demo: sample businesses and prices, bookings kept in the browser, and a WhatsApp handoff, with no accounts, server or payment provider.

    Problem
    Lusaka salons, clinics and barbers need a booking page that works on a phone.
    Role
    I wrote this static demo. The sample brand is Chipo's Braids & Beauty, Kabulonga. The business, staff, prices and bookings are fictional.
    Stack
    HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Bookings stay in the browser. There is no account system, no server and no payment provider.
    Outcome
    A visitor picks a sample service, an optional staff member, a date and a time, then confirms on WhatsApp. Prices are labelled example, and the mobile-money step is marked Demo and charges nothing.

    ZedDrive Motors

    Problem

  • Notebook

    Lusaka Food Prices Analysis

    Analyses World Food Programme retail prices for maize, mealie meal and fuel with pandas and matplotlib/seaborn, one question at a time, keeping each comparison in a single unit and price type.

    Problem
    White maize and mealie meal are everyday foods in Zambia. The notebook asks how Lusaka retail prices have moved, one question at a time.
    Role
    This is a first data-analyst portfolio project. I wrote the notebook.
    Stack
    Python, pandas and matplotlib/seaborn. The file is World Food Programme retail prices, in ZMW only. A kilogram, a 20 L tin and a 25 kg bag are never averaged together.
    Outcome
    The executed notebook reports Lusaka white maize at 0.86 ZMW/kg in 2003 and 3.74 ZMW/kg in 2021. The kwacha price rose far more than the dollar price. Per-kilogram maize quotes for Lusaka end in August 2022.
