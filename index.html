About

I study Computer Science at NIPA in Lusaka. I founded D3VCorp, and I am President of the ICTAZ (Information Communication Technology Association of Zambia) NIPA Student Chapter.

D3VCorp builds websites, mobile apps and systems for businesses. I also do system design: planning architecture, databases, scalability and security.

I am a student founder, and this site is a record of work in progress.

Projects

Two of these are live site demos. The other three are data analysis notebooks on GitHub.

  • Demo

    ZedDrive Motors

    A mobile-first demo website for a fictional Lusaka car dealer that sells and rents cars. It is a portfolio piece to show what a fast, WhatsApp-first site can look like. ZedDrive Motors is fictional. The cars and contact details on that site are sample data.

    Live demo of ZedDrive Motors Repository for ZedDrive Motors

  • Demo

    BookIt Lusaka

    A mobile-first booking page template for small Lusaka businesses: salons, braiders, clinics and barbers. It is a static demo with no account system, no server and no payment provider. The sample brand is Chipo's Braids & Beauty, Kabulonga. The business, staff, prices and bookings are fictional.

    Live demo of BookIt Lusaka Repository for BookIt Lusaka

  • Notebook

    Lusaka road safety

    A beginner portfolio project: one Jupyter notebook on public crash tables for Lusaka Province, 2016–2024. It cleans the labels and answers plain questions with pandas and matplotlib/seaborn charts. The tables are yearly and quarterly totals, not one row per crash.

    Repository for Lusaka road safety

  • Notebook

    Lusaka food prices

    A first data-analysis portfolio project. The notebook answers plain questions about white maize and mealie meal prices from retail markets monitored by the World Food Programme, using pandas and matplotlib/seaborn.

    Repository for Lusaka food prices

  • Notebook

    Lusaka air quality

    A beginner notebook on daily air quality in Lusaka from 1 August 2022 to 18 February 2026. The values are modelled estimates from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API (CAMS global). They are not measurements from ground monitors in Lusaka, and they are not an official health report.

    Repository for Lusaka air quality
