About

I am in the final year of a Bachelor of Computer Science at the National Institute of Public Administration (NIPA) in Lusaka. My foundation is programming, networking, databases and IT support.

Expected graduation is 2027. Coursework includes Mobile Programming, Parallel Systems, and Computer Ethics and Profession. I completed a Grade 12 Certificate at Woodlands A Secondary School, Lusaka, in 2021.

As President of the ICTAZ (Information Communication Technology Association of Zambia) NIPA Student Chapter, I set priorities, guide members, and take technology programmes, workshops and events from planning to delivery with classmates and partners. I advocate for technology awareness and student engagement, building teamwork, communication and organisational skills that transfer to professional IT work.

    Lusaka road safety

    A beginner portfolio project: one Jupyter notebook on public crash tables for Lusaka Province, 2016–2024. It cleans the labels and answers plain questions with pandas and matplotlib/seaborn charts. The tables are yearly and quarterly totals, not one row per crash.

    Repository for Lusaka road safety

  • Notebook

    Lusaka air quality

    A beginner notebook on daily air quality in Lusaka from 1 August 2022 to 18 February 2026. The values are modelled estimates from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API (CAMS global). They are not measurements from ground monitors in Lusaka, and they are not an official health report.

    Repository for Lusaka air quality
